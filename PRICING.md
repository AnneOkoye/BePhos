# Regional pricing plan (to test, not to launch)

Everything here is a hypothesis to test with pilot members who keep coming back. Nothing is built: the pilot stays free. Nothing that marks progress or effort is for sale.

## Why regional

One global price doesn't work for BePhos's first users. Two reference points:

- Focusmate, the closest paid product, charges $8 a month billed yearly or $12 billed monthly, with 3 free sessions a week ([Focusmate](https://www.focusmate.com/pricing)).
- Spotify Premium costs about ₦1,300 to ₦1,600 a month in Nigeria ([Interspace](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/spotify-premium-naira-african-streaming-economics-2026/), [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/05/25/spotify-on-nigerias-streaming-economy/)), against $11.99 in the US. At the official rate of about ₦1,334 to the dollar on 21 October 2026 ([TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/dollar-to-naira-exchange-rate-today-october-21-2026)), that is roughly $1.00 to $1.20, or 8 to 10% of the US price. Big subscription products already price Nigeria at a small fraction of the US price.

## Tiers

Group countries by the World Bank's income groups, which are set by income per person ([2026–27 thresholds](https://www.shankariasparliament.com/current-affairs/world-bank-income-classification-2026)). Use the country of the payment card, not the internet connection, so a VPN doesn't change the price.

| Tier | Countries | Monthly test price | Yearly test price |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | High income (for example the UK, US, EU, Gulf) | $5 | $40 |
| B | Upper-middle income | $2.50 | $20 |
| C | Lower-middle and low income (for example Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, India) | Local currency, about $1 to $1.50. Nigeria: ₦1,500 | Nigeria: ₦12,000 |

Tier A sits below Focusmate on purpose: BePhos is unproven and has fewer sessions. Raise it only if people pay readily.

Show prices only in the local currency, with no conversions on the page.

## What Paystack would take (Nigeria)

Paystack charges 1.5% + ₦100 on local cards, capped at ₦2,000, with the ₦100 waived under ₦2,500. International cards are 3.9% + ₦100 ([Paystack pricing](https://paystack.com/pricing)).

| Price | Paystack fee | You receive |
|---|---|---|
| ₦1,500 a month, local card | ₦22.50 (the ₦100 is waived) | ₦1,477.50 |
| ₦12,000 a year, local card | ₦280 | ₦11,720 |

Paystack supports recurring plans, monthly and yearly among them ([Paystack subscriptions](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/)). It lists Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria and South Africa on its pricing page. Outside those countries you'd need a second payment provider, so check which ones accept a business registered where yours is.

## What would be free, and what might be paid

| Always free | Possibly paid (test which people want) |
|---|---|
| Goals, plans, check-ins, recovery, weekly review | More session times each week |
| Your cohort's weekly session during the pilot | Private small groups with people you invite |
| Your light and fire, earned and chosen | A fuller history and a yearly summary of your record |
| Block, report, download and delete your data | Priority places in popular sessions |

Don't decide the paid list in advance. Ask returning members which part they would miss if it went, then put a price on that.

## Organisations

Scholarship programmes, mentoring groups and university career teams may pay for a whole cohort, so members don't each need a card. Don't set a price yet. In 3 to 5 conversations, ask what they spend now to run accountability sessions, and what a 12-week cohort is worth to them. Sell a structured service, never a promise of scholarship outcomes.

## How to test

1. After week 2 of the pilot, offer the people who returned a place in the next cohort at their tier's price, paid up front. A real payment, not a survey answer.
2. Count: how many were offered, how many paid, and in which tier. Write the counts down, not just percentages.
3. Ask the ones who didn't pay what stopped them: the price, the value, or the timing.
4. Offer a few sponsored places for people who can't pay, and record how many are used.

## Rules to keep

- Easy to cancel, with a reminder before every yearly renewal.
- No countdown timers, fake scarcity or pre-ticked boxes.
- Refund anyone who asks within 7 days of a charge during the pilot.
