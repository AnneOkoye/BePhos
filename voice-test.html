Help us pick a voice service

You’ll join a voice-only room twice, once on each service, for about 10 minutes each. Nothing is recorded and there’s no video. At the end, copy your results and send them back.

Your browser will ask to use the microphone. It stays off until you hold the talk button.

How to run the test

  1. Join with a friend or another tester at the same time, ideally in a different country.
  2. Stay about 10 minutes on Service A. Take turns talking for a minute each.
  3. Rate how voices sounded, then tap Leave.
  4. Do the same on Service B.
  5. Copy your results and send them to the BePhos team.

We measure delay, jitter and lost audio from your browser. We don’t record or keep anyone’s voice.
